## convex-schema-sizing-modes (completed)
Added missing sizing mode fields to Convex templateElementValidator in schema.ts and templates.ts.

Fields added:
- widthMode, widthPercent (element width sizing)
- heightSizingMode, heightSizingPercent (element height sizing)
- heightMode, heightPercent (layout container height)
- flexBasis, gridColumn, gridRow (flex/grid props)
- displayMode, grid config in layoutConfigValidator

Files changed:
- packages/backend/convex/schema.ts
- packages/backend/convex/templates.ts
